# Operating BEAM Deck

Open the real bar icon, its configured bar-position shortcut, or `omarchy-shell nshkr.beam-deck open`. All use the same native panel. The main title and Cockpit / Investigate / Refresh / Shortcuts / Close rail keep their native layout.

## Orient and follow evidence

Start in Cockpit for host census and the selected node. OS visibility and authenticated OTP visibility are different; an undistributed process cannot expose OTP internals. In Investigate → Triage, read provider quality and capture age before interpreting an empty incident list. Unknown or stale evidence is not healthy zero activity.

A finding shows severity, context, evidence class, measured summary and evidence age. Expand it and follow its exact frame or focused inspection action. Nearby events are temporal association, not demonstrated cause. Resource forecasts require qualified measured trends and meaningful hard limits; binary/ETS growth has no fabricated exhaustion time.

Activity filters work over retained local evidence. Use Connections for opened and disconnected observations, or Restarts / name replacement to separate VM creation changes from a name resolving to another PID. Neither a capped scan nor a disconnected distribution link proves a process exited. View captured frame follows the original frame ID; expired evidence stays unavailable.

## Check an intervention

Capture a baseline in Triage before making a deliberate change. It records the helper session, exact frame, capture quality and chosen node incarnation. Replace or clear it explicitly. Compare with latest fixes endpoint B at the frame current when you clicked; the result does not keep moving. Export comparison hands off that exact range.

Read signed bytes/counts and percentage points (`pp`) separately. Reductions/second require distinct comparable deep samples and are not CPU percentages. Restarts or unknown identity disable continuous process/counter comparisons. Consider whether workload changed before attributing improvement to your intervention. Retention expiry and helper restart make a baseline unavailable; they never silently choose another frame.

## Inspect trends and entities

In Flight recorder, choose host RSS or a node's memory, run queue, scheduler utilization or hard-capacity occupancy. Traces share explicit units/scales. Stroke patterns and legends distinguish traces without relying only on color. Gaps, unavailable samples and VM boundaries break lines. Points use captured monotonic positions; a legacy frame without that metadata is explicitly spaced by sequence.

Click or drag to freeze a range. Left/Right steps B; Shift+Left/Right steps A; Home/End reaches first/last. View A/B loads exact retained evidence; Compare A → B compares it. Go live or G releases the frozen view. Switching through Cockpit preserves investigation context; focused editors retain normal modified-key behavior. Omarchy's native H/J/K/L and panel navigation remain in use.

Process and ETS filters only search the capped captured rows. Focused remote requests are deliberate buttons. Inspect exact PID does not follow registered-name replacement. Ancestor and owned-table pivots have Inspection back navigation. A watch of a registered name intentionally follows replacement; stale/deferred/unavailable watch rows cannot authorize current inspection or GC. No mailbox contents, full dictionary, arbitrary application state, binary identifiers/content or ETS keys/values are collected.

## Act and recover

Review scheduler proposals or GC risk in a fresh live view. Historical mode is read-only at both UI and daemon. An unkept trial has a short lease and rolls back when live permission is revoked or the panel closes. Keep/Revert stay available through the safety owner under ordinary job saturation. Keep retains the original-value Restore capability.

Read pending, applied, uncertain and rollback-failed states literally. A timeout does not prove absence of a side effect. An external conflicting scheduler value is not overwritten. A restarted node is not the original restoration target. SIGKILL, partition and host loss limit recovery guarantees. Deep Events is explicit and temporary; it is not a prerequisite for ordinary inspection. Remsh is a deliberate privileged shell to the selected trusted node.

## Share a capped handoff

Export diagnostics or a selected comparison. The owner-only ZIP contains an operator text report, metadata, current live companions and the selected recorder frames. A historical report identifies its range and uses captured findings/watch/activity context; current companion filenames identify their different capture scope. Review operational identities before sharing. No automatic upload or partial presentation-privacy switch is provided.

## Find work and growth during an interval

Open Process and choose **Measure process activity · 5s**. Sort by **Reductions/s**, **Memory change** or **Mailbox change**. This compares two admitted census passes, so a process with a large lifetime counter but little new work no longer dominates the interval ranking. Filter locally, use Previous/Next rows, and select **Inspect PID** to jump to its inspector. First-only/last-only rows have no invented zero baseline; short-lived work between samples can be missed.

For a selected PID, choose **Sample this PID · 5s**. Read status counts alongside the frequency bars and expand a stack to see its argument-free call path. A waiting stack sampled repeatedly is not proof that it consumed CPU; these observations cannot attribute allocations or short function calls. Memory/mailbox changes and reductions/s are separate measured evidence.

In ETS lens, choose **Measure ETS growth · 5s**. Sort by memory or element change and select **Inspect owner**. Matching uses the table identifier and VM incarnation; name reuse is not continuity. An owner change is shown separately. Missing rows can reflect a racing or incomplete scan.

Each workflow has **Cancel** and **Copy measurements**. Closing the panel or entering historical mode cancels acquisition; changing target/session discards obsolete reply handles. Results have capture times and expire with session job retention. These reports are not automatically inserted into the recorder or diagnostic ZIP.
